For the god Ḫaldi, the (or, resp., his) Lord, Argišti, son of Minua, built this building, and a fortress to perfection.
I gave it the name Erbuni; as protection of the Bia lands, and for the intimidation of the enemy land.
Through the greatness of the god Ḫaldi (I am) Argišti, son of Minua, strong king, king of the Bia lands, lord of Ṭušpa-City.
