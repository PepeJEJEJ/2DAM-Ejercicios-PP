By the greatness of the God Khaldi, Argishti, son of Menua, 
built this mighty stronghold and proclaimed it Erebuni 
for the glory of Biainili (Urartu) and to instill fear among the king's enemies. 
Argishti says: The land was a desert, before the great works I accomplished upon it. 
By the greatness of Khaldi, Argishti, son of Menua, is a mighty king, king of Biainili, and ruler of Tushpa
